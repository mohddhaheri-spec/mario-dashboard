The Bale Mountains 🏔️

Ethiopia's misty rooftop & the Afro-alpine wonderland

So… imagine a sky-high meadow where wolves trot, monkeys lounge, and the air is thin enough to make you dizzy. 😄

The Bale Mountains sit in southeastern Ethiopia, about 400 km from Addis Ababa. They're part of a national park famous for the Sanetti Plateau — one of the highest year-round drivable roads in Africa — and the lush, mysterious Harenna Forest tumbling down the southern slopes.

It's a place of two worlds: frosty alpine moorland up top 🥶 and a steamy cloud forest down below ☁️🌳. Locals also call it a giant water tower — rivers that feed millions of people start right here. 💧
